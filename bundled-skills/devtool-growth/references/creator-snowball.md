# Creator Snowball: getting reposted (the #1 boom lever)

## The mechanic (it's real)
Creators are engagement-maximizers: give them content that earns *them* engagement and they amplify it; their audience snowballs to you. BUT it is **bottom-up, give-first, and EARNED, never asked.** You never "make" anyone repost; you build an artifact so good that resharing makes *them* look early/smart, and you've already engaged authentically so your handle is familiar. The bottleneck is *amplification*, not exposure, and amplification is earned by the artifact, not requested.

## The honest math (set expectations)
Solo, unknown, small account, 3 to 5 hrs/week: one good 3-week cycle ≈ pre-warm ~20 micro accounts → ~3 to 5 replies → **1 to 3 genuine reposts IF the demo is undeniable (0 if mediocre)** → low-thousands of qualified impressions → low-single-digit *real* new users. It **compounds over months**; it does **not** produce a viral moment on command, and stars will rise from listings while installs stay flat. Don't mistake that for success. Optimize on install-intent reach, not vanity.

## The tiered ladder (start at Tier 0/1, trade upward)

**Tier 0, Curators / directories (DO FIRST; merit-gated, no relationship, durable install-intent traffic).** Awesome-lists + directories (see `channels.md`). Open clean PRs following each repo's CONTRIBUTING exactly; list under the right category with a one-line value prop + the demo GIF. Zero ego cost, one-time, compounds, auto-propagates to mirrors. After a merge, @-mention the maintainer **once** (template #1).

**Tier 1, Nano/micro (the TRUE first rung; small enough to notice a solo builder).** Individual skill/tool authors + micro reviewers + the **live long-tail = whoever is replying to / quote-tweeting THIS WEEK's launches** (found live, not from a static list). ~80% of your X budget lives here: reply EARLY (<15 min) and ADDITIVELY on their posts for 1 to 2 weeks, peer-to-peer, never a pitch. These produce the first repost you trade upward.

**Tier 2, Mid (the real amplification rung; attainable with prior engagement + a strong artifact).** Practitioner YouTubers (highest single-shot boom probability) + mid X builders + niche newsletters + the platform's own DevRel/PM engineers (substance only). **YouTubers are REACTIVE**, they source from what's already trending on X/Reddit/GitHub, so seed the demo on X first and let them discover it warm. Frame your tool in the platform's own vocabulary so it reads as an *extension*, not a competitor.

**Tier 3, Large (aspirational capstone; NEVER cold-pitch; reached only by organic bubble-up).** The realistic one is **the platform's Head of DevRel, the large account whose actual job is amplifying ecosystem builders.** Earn it: weeks of substantive replies on their threads + ONE crisp demo (repo link in a *reply*, not the main tweet, to avoid reach throttling), tagged sparingly. Essayists (e.g. swyx-tier) are reached via a *written* think-piece in the rising-term lane, not a GIF. Chroniclers (simonw-tier) find tools via GitHub-trending/HN, never via pitch.

## The sequence
1. **Week 1:** build the ONE undeniable demo → embed as README hero → ship Tier-0 PRs in parallel (the part you fully control, cheapest install-intent traffic, zero ban risk).
2. **Weeks 1 to 4:** run the reply ladder (~20 to 30 min/day), map 15 to 25 Tier 1/2 accounts, reply early + additively, pre-warm relationships *before* any artifact lands. 70 to 80% of X effort here, 20 to 30% original posting.
3. **Week 2:** ignition, post the demo natively (no ask, link in a reply) + a value-first show-and-tell in the niche subreddit (only with prior comment history).
4. **Weeks 2 to 3:** convert the first micro-repost into a cascade, thank + add a fresh detail in replies, quote-tweet to reset it in feeds, and DM the next tier up citing the repost as social proof.
5. **Week 3+:** feed the YouTube/newsletter rung (the demo is now warm in their sourcing path); offer micro-reviewers early access.
6. **Ongoing:** newsjack hot "my agent went rogue / looped forever / [platform release]" threads, reply early with the clip, only when you add genuine signal.
7. **Months 2 to 3:** capstone reach (earned), a rising-term essay for the essayists; let momentum + listings surface you to the big DevRel account organically. Repeat the 3-week cycle; it compounds across cycles, not in one push.

## Outreach templates (short, human, non-begging)
- **Curator @-mention (post-merge, NOT a request):** "Thanks for adding [tool] to [list], here's the 30s of why it exists: [clip]. [one-line value prop]. [license], installs as a skill. Happy to answer anything."
- **"Built this for you" (only after they voiced the exact problem, and you've engaged before):** "You posted last week about [their exact complaint], I built [tool] to fix exactly that. 30s clip: [link]. Early access if useful, zero ask."
- **Additive reply (the daily engine):** "[specific build on their actual point]. Hit this building [tool], solved it by [one concrete technique]. Short clip if useful: [link]." Drop the link ONLY when it genuinely fits.
- **Cascade upward (trade a repost as social proof):** "[name], [account who reposted] just shared this and it's landing with the [niche] crowd. Figured you'd want an early look before it's everywhere: [clip]. No ask."
- **Platform eng/DevRel substance reply:** "This maps to something I ran into. [tool] does [X] on top of [their model/vocabulary]. Before/after: [clip]. Curious whether that matches how you'd frame it."
- **Micro-reviewer early access:** "You do the most honest [niche] tool reviews, want early access? [one-line]. 60s before/after: [clip]. No strings, review it however you find it, rough edges included."
- **Newsletter guest tip:** "Reproducible test case if useful: here's [a failing case] [link], and here's [tool] fixing it [link], you can run both. Happy to write up the 3-paragraph technique if it fits an issue."

## Instagram / TikTok for a CLI/dev tool: HARD NO
Wrong-audience, broken-funnel trap. Devs rank IG ~6th (≈20% ever-use vs X ~46%, YouTube ~43%; install-relevant figure ≈ 0). Three kill-factors stack: (1) **intent**, devs are in leisure mode on IG, not tool-evaluation; (2) **link friction**, IG strips clickable links, breaking "saw it → installed it"; (3) **format**, a terminal recording earns IG creators zero algorithmic engagement, so the snowball can't ignite. The thesis is right, the surface is wrong; it only fires where there's install intent + a clickable path (X + YouTube). Only sanctioned IG use: ~2-min passive repurposing of a clip you already made. Never originate IG-first, never pitch IG influencers, never run IG app-install ads (those optimize for mobile-app installs, structurally mismatched to a CLI/skill).

## Ethical / ban lines (non-negotiable, devs detect fakery instantly)
No bought followers · no engagement pods / RT-for-RT rings (X suspends + 14 to 30 day reach suppression) · no astroturf/sockpuppets (FTC fined >$600K for review manipulation) · no undisclosed paid promo · no mass-tagging/mass-DM "pls RT" · no spraying templated outreach (specificity is the whole mechanism) · honor Reddit's ~90/10 self-promo norm · stars are vanity, judge every action on install-intent reach · **every repost must be earned by a real artifact + real prior engagement.**
